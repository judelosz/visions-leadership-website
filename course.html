FAQs

Before you enroll.

Who is this course for?

It is for people who want to better understand anger, identify emotional triggers and warning signs, practice healthier communication and coping strategies, and create a personal plan for continued growth.

How do I get the most from the course?

Move through the modules in order, complete the activities honestly, use the journal alongside the lessons, complete the applicable quizzes, and give yourself time to reflect and practice the strategies.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. The program is educational and coaching-based. It is not medical treatment, psychotherapy, or a substitute for licensed clinical services.

Will a certificate be accepted by a court, employer, or agency?

The Certificate of Completion documents completion of this educational program. Acceptance by a court, probation department, employer, school, or other organization is determined by that organization.

Are coaching sessions included?

No. Individual and group coaching sessions are separate purchases and are not included with the $300 course.

Can an organization enroll a group?

Yes. Organizations can request group enrollment and receive weekly emailed course-progress reports for approved contacts. Contact Visions to discuss the appropriate arrangement and pricing.
