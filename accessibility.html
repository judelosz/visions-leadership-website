Accessibility

Visions wants this website and its information to be usable by as many people as possible.

The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, text alternatives for meaningful images, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts. Visions continues to review the site against WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Need another format?

If you have difficulty using the site or need information in another format, email visionsdba@gmail.com or call 248-793-5060. Please identify the page or information you need and the best way to contact you.
